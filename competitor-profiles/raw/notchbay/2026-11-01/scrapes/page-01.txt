L162: FAQ
L163: ## Asked, answered.
L164: 
L165: Is there a Dynamic Island for Mac?
L166: 
L167: Not natively, macOS doesn't ship one. NotchBay brings a Dynamic Island for macOS (often searched as "Mac OS") to the MacBook notch: live activities for music, calendar, calls and AirPods, plus a clipboard tray and on-device dictation. For the full landscape, including free options, read our 【23†macOS Dynamic Island guide】.
L168: 
L169: Does it work on Macs without a notch, or on external displays?
L170: Yes. On notchless Macs and external displays NotchBay runs as a floating island at the top of the screen, it never draws a fake notch over your display.
L171: 
L172: Is NotchBay a subscription?
L173: 
L174: No. NotchBay is a one-time purchase with every update included. Buy once on the site, your license key arrives by email, and it unlocks one Mac for good.
L175: 
L176: Can it really control Zoom and Google Meet?
L177: 
L178: Yes: mute, stop video, or leave the call straight from the notch, with a live timer, even while you're working in another app.
L179: What does the Tray keep?
L180: 
L181: Whatever you copy: text, links, colors, images, files, plus screenshots and screen recordings as you take them, auto-tagged by type. It holds your latest 60 clips (pin what matters and it never expires), and screenshots are OCR'd so the text inside them is searchable.
L182: 
L183: Is it private and safe to install?
L184: Yes. NotchBay is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Your content stays on your Mac: no accounts, and clips, transcripts and events are never uploaded. Each permission (calendar, microphone, screen recording) is requested only when you turn on the feature that needs it, and dictation uses Apple's on-device speech engine, so audio never leaves your Mac. The app sends one anonymous install check-in (a random id, app version and license state) with no personal data.
