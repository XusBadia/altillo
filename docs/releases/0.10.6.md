## What’s new

- **Your latest file, one less click away.** After adding something to the shelf, the next general opening of Altillo takes you straight to Files. Dropped files also show up on that section as soon as they arrive.
- **Open where the activity is.** With an empty shelf, Altillo opens the section shown by an active indicator beside the notch. When both sides have content, it follows the existing activity priority; without a clear priority, it keeps your last section. Clicking an indicator or using a section's shortcut still opens exactly what you chose.

## Install or update

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Altillo to Applications. Existing
installations can use **Altillo → Check for Updates…**. Altillo is signed with
Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Known limitations

- Apps that open one of their own windows (Google Drive, say) keep it where they put it.
- Showing icons as they look in the menu bar needs Screen Recording; without it the Drawer shows each app's icon.

## Need to roll back?

Quit Altillo, replace it with the DMG from version 0.10.5 and disable automatic
updates temporarily in Settings. Your local Altillo data is kept unless you
explicitly remove it.

## Support

If something fails, use **Altillo → About → Report a Problem** and include the
version, build and redacted diagnostics.
