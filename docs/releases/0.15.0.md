## What’s new

- **Home: choose what each room shows.** In **Settings → Home → Add from a room**, the eye next to a room hides its chip in the notch, and the eye next to a device keeps it out of that room’s view. Your favourites and pages still show it. You can also right-click a room’s chip in the notch to hide it, or a tile to hide it in that room.
- **Home: your own pages.** Create pages like “Night” or “Office” in **Settings → Home**, add tiles to each one from your rooms, and switch between them from the chips in the notch. Each page keeps its own names, icons and sizes.
- **Cursor:** team plans show their model quotas even without a spending limit.

## What’s fixed

- Settings → Home keeps your tiles visible while Home Assistant is loading or unreachable, and says why your devices aren’t listed instead of hiding the section.
- The Drawer’s settings explain when macOS still has an old Accessibility permission for Altillo.

## Install or update

Use **Altillo → Check for Updates…** or download the signed, notarized DMG below.

## Rollback and support

To roll back, quit Altillo, install [0.14.2](https://github.com/XusBadia/altillo/releases/tag/v0.14.2) and temporarily turn off automatic updates in **Settings → General**. For help, use **Altillo → About → Report a Problem** and include the version and redacted diagnostics.
