## What’s new

- **Every Drawer icon opens from its button.** Menus and panels open right under the icon you clicked, with the notch still open. Panels that would draw under the notch open just below it.
- **No more dead clicks.** A click is never dropped while another icon is opening, a panel that won't close no longer blocks the rest, and clicking another Drawer icon while a menu is open switches to it, like the menu bar.
- **Clear answers instead of silence.** If macOS keeps an app's icon out of the menu bar, the Drawer says so. Apps that restart are picked up again before their icon opens.
- **Ears that fit what they say.** The resting notch's ears grow to their content, say what's going on in words, and an empty ear shows what matters next.

## Install or update

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Altillo to Applications. Existing
installations can use **Altillo → Check for Updates…**. Altillo is signed with
Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Known limitations

- Apps that open one of their own windows (Google Drive, say) keep it where they put it.
- Showing icons as they look in the menu bar needs Screen Recording; without it the Drawer shows each app's icon.

## Need to roll back?

Quit Altillo, replace it with the DMG from version 0.10.3 and disable automatic
updates temporarily in Settings. Your local Altillo data is kept unless you
explicitly remove it.

## Support

If something fails, use **Altillo → About → Report a Problem** and include the
version, build and redacted diagnostics.
