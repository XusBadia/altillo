Privacy policy

Your things stay on your Mac.

Altillo has no analytics, advertising or telemetry service. It does not send us your files, calendar, clipboard, camera image, agent sessions, Ask conversations or credentials.

Version 1.2 · effective 15 October 2026

What stays local

Altillo stores its settings in macOS preferences and the content you ask it to remember in its Application Support folder. This can include shelf copies, notes and their history, timers, optional clipboard history, saved Ask items, usage snapshots and agent-hook installation records. Calendar, media and camera access use macOS permissions and remain on the device. Removing an item inside Altillo removes Altillo’s copy; retention of originals remains under your control.

Drawer and menu bar icons

Drawer needs Accessibility permission to list menu bar icons and open their menus. Screen Recording is optional: with it, Altillo captures only the menu bar to show the actual icons instead of app icons. It keeps the cropped icon images on your Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/Altillo/MenuBarGlyphs; it does not save the full menu bar image or upload captures. Hidden icons use their last saved image. Cached images have no automatic expiry and remain until replaced by a new capture or you delete that folder. Without Screen Recording, Drawer still works using app icons and system symbols.

AI usage checks

When you enable usage checks, Altillo reads the sign-in or API credential already stored by the provider’s own app or CLI and presents it only to that same provider to request quota information. Altillo does not refresh, rewrite or retain a separate copy of those credentials. It uses ephemeral sessions without a persistent cookie or disk cache.

Ask and web lookups

Ask runs on-device by default. Web access is off until you allow it for one question or in Settings. A short search derived from the question may then go to DuckDuckGo, with Bing as a fallback; weather place names go to Open-Meteo; and Wikipedia may be used as a reference fallback. Result pages selected by the search may be fetched to prepare the answer. Requests use ephemeral sessions without an Altillo account or advertising cookies. Those services receive the request and apply their own privacy policies.

Direct mail accounts (in development)

R61 is developing Google, Microsoft, iCloud and IMAP accounts with reading, send/reply, read/archive/trash actions and attachments. It is not included in 0.13.0. The earlier read-only tests do not validate this expansion, and real-provider and distribution validation is pending. Google uses identity, gmail.modify (restricted; also permits sending); Microsoft uses delegated User.Read, Mail.ReadWrite and Mail.Send. iCloud uses a two-factor-protected app-specific password; other IMAP accounts use the server credentials you provide. IMAP requires TLS, and SMTP requires implicit TLS or mandatory STARTTLS before authentication. Account identity and credentials stay in this Mac’s private, non-synchronizing Keychain; Microsoft manages tokens in its private MSAL cache. Requests go directly to your selected provider, not an Altillo mail server.

Up to 50 Inbox summaries per account and bounded bodies stay in memory during the unlocked session. The five-minute limit applies to arrival notices, not the Inbox. HTML becomes text without fetching images, scripts or remote resources; mail content is not sent to Ask. Reading does not mark server mail as read; explicit actions can change it, archive it or move it to Trash. Altillo does not permanently delete mail or empty Trash; provider retention policies still apply. Sending shares your chosen content, recipients and attachments with the provider and recipients. The compose interface allows 18 MiB of attachments in total and a 25 MiB complete MIME message. Providers can retain Sent copies; Graph may leave an unsent server draft if an upload fails. For iCloud/IMAP, saving a Sent copy via IMAP is on by default after SMTP accepts the message; turn it off if your server already saves copies. If only saving that copy fails, Altillo reports the message as sent and warns you not to resend. An uncertain send is never retried automatically: check your provider’s Sent folder and, if needed, the recipient before repeating.

Attachments download only on request, up to 20 MiB decoded, to a save path you choose. These persistent files are outside the transient mail cache. Lock, sleep or disabling Mail clears the local Inbox, reader and composer and stops requests; pausing retains credentials. Disconnecting removes local account credentials, without claiming remote revocation. Neither action removes saved downloads, provider drafts, Sent copies or recipients’ messages. Opening originals or links opens your browser only after your action. The Apple Mail bridge remains a mutually exclusive alternative.

Mail (optional beta)

Mail is off by default. When you enable it and create the Altillo Mail rule in Apple Mail, that rule sends new messages to a native helper through a local pipe and Unix socket. Altillo keeps the sender, subject, account name and a short beginning of the body in memory for up to five minutes to show the message and recognize access codes or sign-in links. It does not save a mail archive, send mail content to Ask, or upload it to an Altillo server. Your sender/account filters are stored locally. Disabling Mail, locking the screen or putting the Mac to sleep clears the recent messages. The code button marks its clipboard entry as private; other clipboard apps follow their own policies. Opening a sign-in link contacts its website in your browser only after you click it. Before uninstalling, disable Mail and remove the Altillo Mail rule in Apple Mail Settings. Disconnecting in Altillo stops reception and preserves the scripts and user-created rules. After removing every rule that uses them, use Show scripts in Altillo Settings to remove only Altillo Mail.scpt and Altillo Mail Bridge.scpt manually. Altillo checks its local script files and confirms reception from a new delivery; it does not query Mail rules or require an Altillo → Mail Automation grant.

Agents and local integrations

Agent hooks communicate with Altillo through a local Unix socket. Altillo shows permission requests and relays only your explicit choice; it never auto-approves. OpenCode integration may connect to its loopback server on your Mac. Hook configuration and backups stay locally in Application Support, and “Prepare to uninstall” previews every change before removal.

Music and lyrics

Now Playing reads the media session already available on your Mac and may download artwork from the URL supplied by the active media app. Lyrics stay off until you press “Load Lyrics”. That action sends the current track’s title, artist, album and duration to LRCLIB to find matching lyrics, using an ephemeral session without cookies or an Altillo account. No audio or media library is uploaded.

Updates, links and diagnostics

Checking for updates contacts Altillo’s signed Sparkle feed on GitHub Pages; downloading one contacts GitHub Releases. Opening GitHub, Aurio, a meeting or another external link sends you to that service in your browser. Release logs stay on your Mac and redact credentials, question text, clipboard content and full file paths. “Copy Safe Diagnostics” creates local text containing only the Altillo version, macOS version, architecture and hook status; nothing is uploaded automatically.

Home (optional)

Home is off until you turn it on and connect it. To find a server, Altillo looks for Home Assistant on your local network (Bonjour and homeassistant.local); macOS asks for Local Network access first. Altillo then talks only to the Home Assistant address you connect to, directly from your Mac over your network or your own https URL. Signing in opens your Home Assistant’s own login page in your browser and creates an access token named after your Mac. The token is stored in the macOS Keychain and never leaves your Mac except to authenticate with your own server. Entity names and states are kept in memory only; your favourites (entity ids) are kept in Altillo’s preferences on your Mac. Doorbell camera snapshots are fetched only to show a doorbell peek, kept in memory and never saved. Nothing goes through any Altillo server, because there is none. Disconnecting deletes the token from your Mac; to revoke it, delete it in your Home Assistant profile.

What we collect

Because Altillo has no account, analytics or telemetry backend, we do not receive or retain personal data from normal app use. If you contact us or file a GitHub issue, the information you choose to provide is handled by that communication service and retained as needed to answer or maintain the project.

Your control

  • Disable each module or its macOS permission in Altillo Settings or System Settings.
  • Turn web lookups and optional clipboard history off at any time.
  • Use “Prepare to uninstall” before deleting the app to remove Altillo-owned agent hooks safely.
  • Delete Altillo’s Application Support data to remove its local saved state.
  • Delete ~/Library/Application Support/Altillo/MenuBarGlyphs to remove cached menu bar icons.
