## Hardware-dependent cases not represented by this machine

The following remain unverified rather than being inferred from mocks:

- physical-notch geometry and trackpad gestures on a MacBook;
- macOS 26 Drawer hiding and recovery;
- real Claude/Codex allow, deny, timeout, reply and dangerous-command hold;
- terminal/pane targeting across supported terminal apps;
- Music, Spotify and a third player;
- Finder/Fotos/Mail/browser file promises, drag-out to chat apps, AirDrop and
  Quick Look as physical gestures;
- a real Calendar Join event, camera/TCC granted and denied, VoiceOver spoken
  output, Reduce Motion/Transparency, multi-display Spaces/full-screen/wake.

These limits were included in the 0.7.6 release notes and blocked declaring
plan 009 complete at that time. The later user acceptance is recorded separately;
it does not change the scope of the observations in this historical matrix.
