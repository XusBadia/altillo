## What’s new

- **Home (beta): Home Assistant in your notch.** A new Home section shows your favourite lights, switches, blinds, scenes and sensors as switch-plate tiles that light up in each bulb’s real colour. Click to toggle, drag a light to dim it or a blind to open it partway, and run a scene with one click. Home is off by default: turn it on in **Settings → Home** or from the welcome.
- **Connect in a few clicks.** Altillo finds Home Assistant on your network and signs in through Home Assistant’s own login page in your browser, so no token needs copying. Pasting a long-lived access token remains available, and Altillo checks the address before connecting.
- **Make it yours.** Altillo picks a starting set of favourites for you. Rearrange them in Settings on a grid that mirrors the notch, add more with **Add from a room**, and right-click a tile to rename it, change its icon or size, hide it or open it in Home Assistant. Room chips in the notch show everything in that room.
- **The doorbell peeks out.** Choose a doorbell and, optionally, a camera: when someone rings, the notch shows a glance with the camera image. Home can also sit beside the notch while Altillo is closed.
- **Its own Settings page.** Home joins the sidebar next to the other sections, with the connection, the favourites editor, the doorbell and the list of ways to bring in other devices (Philips Hue, Google Home, Alexa and Matter through Home Assistant; Apple Home scenes through Shortcuts).
- **Private by design.** Altillo talks directly to your Home Assistant over your network; nothing passes through any other server. The token is kept in your Keychain. All new text is available in Spanish.

## Install or update

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Altillo to Applications. Existing
installations can use **Altillo → Check for Updates…**. Altillo is signed with
Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Known limitations

- Home is a beta and supports Home Assistant only. Other platforms work through Home Assistant; on the Mac, Apple Home can only run scenes through Shortcuts and cannot show device states.
- Away from your network, Home needs a secure (https) external address for your Home Assistant; plain http is only used on the local network.
- macOS asks once for Local Network access the first time Altillo looks for Home Assistant.

## Need to roll back?

Quit Altillo, replace it with the DMG from version 0.10.7 and disable automatic
updates temporarily in Settings → About. Your local Altillo data is kept unless
you explicitly remove it.

## Support

If something fails, use **Settings → About → Report a Problem** and include the
version, build and redacted diagnostics.
