L20: AI-Powered Renaming
L21: ### Rename files with on-device AI
L22: Dragin sees what's in your images. Using on-device Apple Intelligence and the Vision framework, it analyzes screenshots and photos to generate descriptive filenames based on actual content — capturing app names from UI screenshots, reading visible text, recognizing objects and scenes, and pulling date and location metadata from photos. Rename individually, batch-process a whole shelf with one click, or optionally rename the originals on disk automatically. No cloud required.
