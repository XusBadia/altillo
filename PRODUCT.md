# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users who move files between apps and use coding agents throughout their working day. They need to understand status and take the next action without leaving their workflow.

## Product Purpose

Altillo turns the Mac notch into a discreet place for temporary files, AI usage, live agents and timely notices. Settings should make each capability discoverable and explain its prerequisites.

## Brand Personality

Warm, native and direct. Follow the established voice in brand-context.md and the existing Desvan components.

## Anti-references

Decorative dashboards, vague status messages, unexplained switches, and controls that promise a connection the app cannot perform.

## Design Principles

- State what is happening and what the user can do next.
- Distinguish connecting an account from enabling usage monitoring.
- Explain dependencies where the user encounters them.
- Keep routine settings compact, with additional guidance available inline.
- Keep credentials on the Mac and describe their use accurately.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native keyboard-accessible controls, meaningful VoiceOver labels, selectable instructions, localized English and Spanish copy, and the existing reduced-motion behavior.
