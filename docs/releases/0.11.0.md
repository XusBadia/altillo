## What’s new

- **Home (beta): Home Assistant in your notch.** A new Home section shows your favourite lights, switches, fans, blinds, scenes, scripts and sensors as switch-plate tiles that light up in each bulb’s real colour. Click to toggle, drag a light to dim it or a blind to open it partway, and run a scene with one click. Hold a light or blind for brightness, white temperature, colour presets or position. Home is off by default: turn it on in **Settings → Home** or from the welcome.
- **Connect in a few clicks.** Altillo finds Home Assistant on your network and signs in through Home Assistant’s own login page in your browser, then keeps a long-lived token named “Altillo (your Mac’s name)” in your Keychain. Pasting a long-lived access token remains available, and Altillo checks the address before connecting.
- **Make it yours.** Altillo picks up to eight favourites to start. Rearrange them in Settings on a grid that mirrors the notch, add more with **Add from a room**, create your own pages, and right-click a tile to rename it, change its icon or size, hide it or open it in Home Assistant. Room chips in the notch show that room’s lights, blinds, switches and scenes.
- **The doorbell peeks out.** Choose a doorbell and, optionally, a camera: when someone rings, the notch shows a glance with a snapshot from the camera. While the notch is closed, Home can also mention a door or window left open for 10 minutes or lights left on when nobody is home.
- **Its own Settings page.** Home joins the sidebar next to the other sections, with the connection, the favourites editor, the doorbell and the ways to bring in other devices: Philips Hue, Google Home, Alexa and Matter through Home Assistant. Apple Home through Shortcuts is marked as coming soon.
- **Private by design.** Altillo talks directly to your Home Assistant, on your local network or at the https address you enter; nothing passes through any other server. All new text is available in Spanish.

## Install or update

Download the DMG below, open it and drag Altillo to Applications. Existing
installations can use **Altillo → Check for Updates…**. Altillo is signed with
Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Known limitations

- Home is a beta, connects to one Home Assistant server and controls lights, switches, fans (on/off), covers, scenes and scripts. Climate, locks, alarm panels, garage doors and media players are not supported yet, and the doorbell shows a still snapshot, not a live stream.
- Other platforms work through Home Assistant. On the Mac, Apple Home cannot show device states; running its scenes from Home through Shortcuts is planned.
- Away from your network, Home needs a secure (https) external address for your Home Assistant; plain http is only used on the local network.
- macOS asks once for Local Network access the first time Altillo looks for Home Assistant.
- **Disconnect** removes the token from your Mac. To revoke it, also delete “Altillo (…)” in Home Assistant under your profile → Security → Long-lived access tokens.

## Need to roll back?

Quit Altillo, replace it with the DMG from version 0.10.7 and disable automatic
updates temporarily in Settings → About. Your local Altillo data is kept unless
you explicitly remove it.

## Support

If something fails, use **Settings → About → Report a Problem** and include the
version, build and redacted diagnostics.
